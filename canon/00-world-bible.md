# World Bible — public canon v0

Curated 2026-10-10 from the brainstorm (`docs/source/chatgpt-brainstorm.md`). Items
marked PROPOSED are not yet decided.

## Premise
The Department is a clandestine federal agency created in 1947 by Continuity Order 7.
Its public identity is the Office of Administrative Survey: records management,
government property inventories, unusual interagency administrative matters. Its actual
mandate: identify, document, contain, and where possible exploit events, objects, persons
and processes that demonstrate persistent deviation from established physical or
historical expectation.

The present is October 1977. The Department is divided over a classified initiative,
Project NIGHTJAR. Senior researcher Dr. Evelyn Marsh has disappeared. Her last known
communication: "Stop correcting the records. The discrepancies are the only things
keeping it outside."

## Tone
Bureaucratic cosmic horror. Institutional realism (carbon copies, memoranda,
authorization forms, teletypes, interdepartmental hostility). Scientific uncertainty.
American gothic (forgotten communities, abandoned facilities, local folklore).
Weaponized information. The horror enters through small inconsistencies: a personnel
file with two dates of death; a field report describing a building that no longer
exists; a call logged from an office closed in 1959.

## Divisions
| Division | Mandate | Institutional interest |
|---|---|---|
| Office of the Director | central authority, appointments, strategic priorities | — |
| Field Operations | investigations, containment, witnesses, surveillance | operational independence |
| Research & Analysis | experiments, interpretation | observe vs. reproduce |
| Records & Archives | cataloguing, classification, preservation, destruction | custody of knowledge |
| Internal Compliance | security, personnel investigations, discipline | leverage over senior officials |
| Special Programs | compartmentalized research; NIGHTJAR believed inside | secrecy, funding |
| Administration | salaries, procurement, transport, facilities, contracts | control of means |

## Factions (cross-divisional)
- **Continuity Committee** — the establishment. Stability, secrecy, institutional authority.
- **Instrumentalists** — anomalies can be understood, replicated, harnessed. Discovery, advantage.
- **Witnesses** — experiments are damaging the integrity of observation. Evidence, preservation, restraint.
- **Disclosure Circle** — the public deserves to know. Disclosure, accountability, decentralization.
- **Quiet Office** — unofficial; membership and purpose unknown. (Secret canon decides what it is.)

Factions recruit, infiltrate, bargain, expel, fracture and merge. Players are never
permanently assigned.

## Governance
The **Continuity Council** has nine voting seats among senior officials and divisional
representatives; a seat may be held by an NPC or a player. Jurisdiction: clearance
policy, appointments, large expenditures, investigations of senior officers, special
operational authority, changes to departmental directives. Older founding directives,
compartmentalized mandates and emergency authorities can restrict it. The exact charter
is the Rules Lawyer's task (`agents/tasks/continuity-charter.md`).

## Evidence tiers
Intelligence (believed relevant, not established) → Evidence (artifact, testimony or
observation with provenance) → Established Finding (accepted by an authority under its
procedures). A finding can be legally established and factually false.

## Correspondence classes
Memoranda (official record, citable) · Private correspondence · Directives (actionable
by recognized authority) · Petitions and motions · Interrogatories (answer, refuse, lie
or contest, with consequences) · Sealed dispatches.

## Rhythm
One round per day. Dispatch 9:00 AM Eastern; order lock 8:00 PM Eastern; adjudication
after lock. Fourteen rounds per episode. PROPOSED for launch: a pilot of Episodes 1–3
with twelve players. Full season: twelve episodes in three acts (Discovery 1–4,
Conspiracy 5–8, Collapse 9–12).

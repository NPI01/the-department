# Personnel Directory — public faces

Public canon v1 (2026-10-11). Agent-team draft (Showrunner → Character Writers → Skeptic →
Archivist), corrected and promoted by Claude. Every entry below is PROPOSED until Nathan
confirms it; the seven canon characters from `canon/secret/03` keep their canon facts.
Hidden allegiances are in `canon/secret/05-allegiance-matrix.md`.

Corrections applied on promotion: Tagus church is St. Olaf; Marsh's cover story is an
out-of-state transfer; department heads are long-serving NPCs, not new hires; in-world
messages are memos and letters, never "email".
*Safe for any prompt that reaches a player. Contains official titles, public reputations, and verifiable surface-level facts.*

### Executive & Internal Staff

**Edmund H. Royce**
*   **Title:** Founder and Chairman
*   **Department:** Executive Office
*   **Public Profile:** Industrial patriarch. Founded Royce Industries in 1959. Publicly benevolent, paternal, and committed to American scientific superiority. Believes Station R is a bulwark against moral decay.
*   **Voice:** Formal, authoritative, uses "we" to refer to the company.
*   **Round 1 Hook:** A welcome memo emphasizing "shared duty" and strict confidentiality regarding the new hires' orientation.

**Dr. Adrian Vale**
*   **Title:** Chief Scientist
*   **Department:** Executive Board / DSRD
*   **Public Profile:** Visionary, gentle, absent-minded. Has been with Royce since 1959. Beloved by junior staff; seen as the only executive who treats employees as humans. Often found in the library or walking the prairie.
*   **Voice:** Warm, slightly rambling, academic. Uses metaphors from nature.
*   **Round 1 Hook:** An invitation to a "casual" discussion about the future of research, hinting that the official orientation is too rigid.

**Harold Sloane**
*   **Title:** Director of Corporate Security
*   **Department:** Corporate Security
*   **Public Profile:** Strict, efficient, unsmiling. Former military intelligence. Runs the campus like a base. Employees fear his attention to detail. Seen as the guardian of secrets.
*   **Voice:** Clipped, bureaucratic, imperative. No adjectives.
*   **Round 1 Hook:** A mandatory security briefing requiring all new hires to sign liability waivers and submit to badge checks.

**Margaret Hensley**
*   **Title:** Chief Financial Officer
*   **Department:** Procurement & Logistics (finance)
*   **Public Profile:** Sharp, practical, concerned with the bottom line. Seen as a "bean counter" who keeps the lights on. Employees avoid her unless they need expense reports approved.
*   **Voice:** Professional, terse, defensive. Uses accounting jargon.
*   **Round 1 Hook:** A request for all new hires to submit expense codes for orientation materials, with a warning about "budgetary discipline."

**Leonard Fisk**
*   **Title:** Senior Security Analyst
*   **Department:** Corporate Security
*   **Public Profile:** Quiet, observant veteran. Sloane’s loyal soldier. Thorough, by-the-book, socially awkward. Spends time reviewing badge logs and CCTV.
*   **Voice:** Formal, concise, slightly paranoid. Uses security terminology.
*   **Round 1 Hook:** An interrogation regarding the new hire’s background, specifically asking about "unusual interests" or "local contacts."

**Dr. Evelyn Marsh**
*   **Title:** Research Scientist (Biological Sciences)
*   **Department:** Biological Sciences
*   **Public Profile:** "Transferred out of state" on September 29, 1977. Official records state a transfer to an out-of-state facility. Colleagues recall her as brilliant but increasingly uneasy.
*   **Voice:** (Archival) Precise, analytical, tense.
*   **Round 1 Hook:** Her empty office is a site of interest. A colleague mentions she left in a hurry.

**Paul Greeley**
*   **Title:** Lab Technician (Deceased)
*   **Department:** Biological Sciences
*   **Public Profile:** Died in an industrial accident in July 1977. Official report cites improper handling of chemicals. Remembered as diligent but clumsy.
*   **Voice:** (Archival) Simple, direct, respectful.
*   **Round 1 Hook:** His locker in Building 2 is sealed. A rumor persists that he "saw something he shouldn't have."

### Department Heads

**Dr. Sarah Ely**
*   **Title:** Director, Biological Sciences
*   **Department:** Biological Sciences
*   **Public Profile:** Young, ambitious evolutionary biologist. Idealistic, eager to prove Royce’s work has medical applications. Runs the HARVEST project.
*   **Voice:** Enthusiastic, academic, slightly frantic. Uses Latin names casually.
*   **Round 1 Hook:** Asks new hires to assist with "routine tissue sampling" in the lab, framing it as a learning opportunity.

**Col. (Ret.) Frank Baines**
*   **Title:** Director, Aerospace Systems
*   **Department:** Aerospace Systems
*   **Public Profile:** Stiff, by-the-book military veteran. Manages the NIGHTJAR project. Respected for discipline, disliked for inflexibility. Views Royce as a partner in national defense.
*   **Voice:** Formal, imperative, dry. Uses rank heavily.
*   **Round 1 Hook:** Demands strict adherence to flight line safety protocols; warns against "unauthorized curiosity" regarding night flights.

**Julian Thorne**
*   **Title:** Director, Information Systems
*   **Department:** Information Systems
*   **Public Profile:** Brilliant, reclusive computer scientist. Speaks in fragments, loathes physical meetings. Manages the mainframe and CHOIR project. Viewed as socially awkward.
*   **Voice:** Detached, technical, cold. Uses terms like "noise" and "signal" for human interactions.
*   **Round 1 Hook:** Sends a cryptic memo about "data integrity" and asks new hires to verify their access credentials immediately.

**Dorothy Armitage**
*   **Title:** Director, Personnel & Administration
*   **Department:** Personnel & Administration
*   **Public Profile:** Meticulous, motherly administrator. With Royce since 1965. Seen as the "conscience" of the company; advocates for employee wellness.
*   **Voice:** Warm, bureaucratic, subtly threatening. Uses "Darling" patronizingly.
*   **Round 1 Hook:** Conducts "wellness interviews" with new hires, asking about their mental state and "beliefs about the unknown."

**Gerald Voss**
*   **Title:** Director, Procurement & Logistics
*   **Department:** Procurement & Logistics
*   **Public Profile:** Gruff, pragmatic, obsessed with supply chains. Known for cutting costs and delaying deliveries. Seen as a necessary evil.
*   **Voice:** Blunt, transactional, impatient.
*   **Round 1 Hook:** Requires new hires to sign off on inventory lists for their departments, including items that don't seem to exist.

**Dr. Elias Thorne** *(Note: Distinct from Julian Thorne)*
*   **Title:** Director, Advanced Materials
*   **Department:** Advanced Materials
*   **Public Profile:** Eccentric metallurgist. Obsessed with ORPHEUS project. Seen as a genius who ignores safety regulations.
*   **Voice:** Excitable, fragmented, uses analogies to music.
*   **Round 1 Hook:** Asks new hires to help move "sensitive prototypes" that are heavier than they look.

**Dr. Aris Kovic**
*   **Title:** Director, Applied Physics
*   **Department:** Applied Physics
*   **Public Profile:** Intense, secretive. Manages the JANUS project. Rarely seen outside the lab. Rumored to be working on energy weapons.
*   **Voice:** Low, urgent, whispery.
*   **Round 1 Hook:** Requests a "calibration check" on a device that hums when touched.

### External Liaisons & Regional Contacts

**Daniel Mercer**
*   **Title:** CIA Technical Liaison
*   **Department:** External (Federal)
*   **Public Profile:** Federal visitor. Evaluates Royce’s ownership and history. Seen as a bureaucratic nuisance by Royce staff. Polite but probing.
*   **Voice:** Polite, detached, precise. Uses passive voice.
*   **Round 1 Hook:** Asks new hires about "company culture" and "who really makes decisions," implying he suspects occult influence.

**Maya Okafor**
*   **Title:** Clerk, Procurement & Logistics
*   **Department:** Procurement & Logistics
*   **Public Profile:** Two years at Station R, in the office that signs for everything that arrives. The one person a new hire can buy lunch for. Has seen two versions of the campus map. Family in Tagus. (PROPOSED, 2026-10-13.)
*   **Voice:** Warm, gossipy, casual. Signs "Maya."

**Walter Crane**
*   **Title:** Outside Director, Board of Royce Industries
*   **Department:** External (Minneapolis)
*   **Public Profile:** The 1969 investor. Attends Board meetings from Minneapolis; reads the Chief Scientist's reports before the Board does. Asks what a thing costs and what it returns. (PROPOSED in 06; added 2026-10-13.)
*   **Voice:** Dry, commercial, courteous. Signs "W. Crane."

**Sheriff Dale Hollis**
*   **Title:** Sheriff, Ward County Sheriff's Department
*   **Department:** External (Law Enforcement)
*   **Public Profile:** 58-year-old lawman. Fixes his own cars, attends football games. Treats Royce with polite distance. Considers them a necessary economic engine.
*   **Voice:** Gruff, concise, weary. Uses "reckon."
*   **Round 1 Hook:** A call to Royce Security about "trespassers" near the perimeter, which turns out to be a misunderstanding.

**Gary Vane**
*   **Title:** Owner, Vane Cattle Co.
*   **Department:** External (Rancher)
*   **Public Profile:** Rugged, fourth-generation rancher. Vocal in local papers about "strange goings-on." Sells hay to the company.
*   **Voice:** Blunt, agricultural, suspicious. Refers to company people as "suitcases."
*   **Round 1 Hook:** Complains to the Sheriff about "lights" over his pasture; refuses to speak to Royce reps.

**Elmer Bernard**
*   **Title:** Retiree / Caretaker, St. Olaf Lutheran Church (Tagus)
*   **Department:** External (Local Resident)
*   **Public Profile:** 72 years old. Last year-round resident of Tagus. Maintains the cemetery. Considered harmless and eccentric; claims to know "old stories."
*   **Voice:** Whispery, archaic, fearful. Pauses as if listening to a frequency.
*   **Round 1 Hook:** A letter to the editor of the local paper about "the ground humming."

**Capt. Thomas Bradley**
*   **Title:** Chief Pilot, Prairie Meridian Aviation
*   **Department:** External (Contractor)
*   **Public Profile:** Former Navy aviator. Runs the night flight contract. Professional, discreet, expensive. Claims flights are "calibration runs."
*   **Voice:** Clipped, technical, cynical. Uses aviation jargon.
*   **Round 1 Hook:** A complaint about "instrument interference" during a recent flight, filed with Royce Aerospace.

**Tech. Sgt. Arthur Doyle**
*   **Title:** Senior Airman, Minot AFB
*   **Department:** External (Military Base)
*   **Public Profile:** Low-level radar technician. Runs the radio club in town. Friendly, eager to be liked. Leaks "harmless" gossip for favors.
*   **Voice:** Chatty, nervous, informal. Over-explains.
*   **Round 1 Hook:** Mentions "ghost signals" on radar to a Royce researcher in a local bar.

**Martin Sayer**
*   **Title:** Private Correspondent
*   **Department:** External (Yonkers, NY)
*   **Public Profile:** Freelance investigative journalist. Writes for niche paranormal magazines. Polite, persistent. No known connection to Royce operations.
*   **Voice:** Passionate, detailed, slightly paranoid. Quotes sources.
*   **Round 1 Hook:** A letter sent to a new hire’s home address, referencing "the pattern" and enclosing a newspaper clipping with a symbol circled.

### Tagus and the county (Episode 4; PROPOSED 2026-10-18)

**Lyle Brandvold**
*   **Title:** Field Surveyor, Prairie Geological Services
*   **Department:** External (Contractor)
*   **Public Profile:** 34, of Minot. Three seasons surveying for PGS at Tagus. Careful, literal, writes everything down. Overdue since the afternoon before Thanksgiving; his wife has been to the sheriff.
*   **Voice:** Flat, literal, exact. Measurements with units. Signs "L. Brandvold."

**Agnes Rud**
*   **Title:** Farmer, west of Tagus
*   **Department:** External (Local Resident)
*   **Public Profile:** 67. Keeps the farm west of the siding alone since her husband died, and the only guest room in Tagus. Has complained twice to the company about surveyors crossing her land at night and never had an answer. Feeds anyone who comes without a form.
*   **Voice:** Plain, unhurried, farm-direct. Asks a question back. Signs "A. Rud."

**Ole Sandvik**
*   **Title:** Formerly Prairie Geological Services
*   **Department:** External (Local Resident)
*   **Public Profile:** Early sixties. Surveyed for PGS at Tagus from the first year and lives in a trailer behind the elevator. Has told the sheriff he will not talk to anyone from the company.
*   **Voice:** Five words or fewer. The weather. Unsigned.

**Curtis Mahlum**
*   **Title:** Formerly Prairie Geological Services
*   **Department:** External (Local Resident)
*   **Public Profile:** Technician for PGS until 1976; now drives a feed truck out of Stanley. Says they were looking for a tunnel, and will say it to anybody who buys the coffee.
*   **Voice:** Garrulous, cheerful; every story has a tunnel. Signs "Curt."

**Dennis Pfeifer**
*   **Title:** Formerly Prairie Geological Services
*   **Department:** External (Local Resident)
*   **Public Profile:** Drove for PGS until August. Says there was never any digging at Tagus and that people should stop asking him. Has a family.
*   **Voice:** Defensive, short, repeats himself. Signs his full name.

**Conrad Ashby**
*   **Title:** Contracting Officer, Defense Supply
*   **Department:** External (Federal)
*   **Public Profile:** A federal contracting officer who writes from an address that is withheld. Polite, exact, and interested in the condition of things the company holds under contract. Addresses only officers of the company.
*   **Voice:** Formal, passive voice, clause numbers. Signs "C. Ashby, Contracting Officer."

### Episode 2 (PROPOSED 2026-10-18)

**Walter Nygaard**
*   **Title:** Custodian, Building 2
*   **Department:** Facilities (nights)
*   **Public Profile:** Night custodian for Buildings 2 and 3 since 1974. Keeps to himself, keeps the floors, and was told after July to hand anything from room 2-C to Security.
*   **Voice:** Quiet, careful, blue-collar polite. Three lines and a misspelling. Signs "W. Nygaard."

**Ruth Greeley**
*   **Title:** Schoolteacher, Fargo
*   **Department:** External (Family)
*   **Public Profile:** Paul Greeley's sister. Received his effects in July and a letter promising a notebook that never came. Has written to the company three times and numbers her letters.
*   **Voice:** Plain, schoolteacher-correct, one direct question per letter. Signs "Ruth Greeley."

### Episode 5 (PROPOSED 2026-10-23)

**Reinhold Kessel**
*   **Title:** Field Supervisor, Prairie Geological Services
*   **Department:** External (Contractor)
*   **Public Profile:** Runs the PGS yard on the Tagus road and its two trucks. Signs the field log, locks the doors, and telephones Procurement when something goes wrong. Has worked the parcel since 1972.
*   **Voice:** Procedural, defensive; quotes the field protocol by paragraph. Signs "R. Kessel, Field Supv."

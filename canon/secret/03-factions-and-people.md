## Four overlapping conspiracies
1. **Scientific** — Vale and associates hide that the apparatus may create what it studies.
2. **Security** — federal sponsors withhold intelligence from Royce; other officials investigate; they trip over each other.
3. **Occult** — Keepers restrain, Ascendants accelerate; both hold history Royce lacks.
4. **Corporate** — directors misrepresent progress, finances, liabilities; some protect, some prepare to profit from collapse.
None controls the others.

## Recruitment
Cult invitations are not a starting choice. Select players receive them after Round 4
based on role, early discoveries, and simulation need. Some are genuine Keeper or
Ascendant approaches; some are Fisk's or Sloane's provocations; some are rival sects.
Responses: attend, report, infiltrate, ignore. Membership is never automatically
betrayal of Royce.

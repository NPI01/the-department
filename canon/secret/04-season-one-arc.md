# Season One Arc — The High Country

SECRET CANON v1 (2026-10-11). Engine and GM only. Reconciles Bible 02 §18 (six-stage
progression), Bible 03 §19 (the April 1978 countdown) and Bible 04 §14 (Tagus per
episode) into one twelve-episode spine. Episodes are crisis generators, not scripts;
each adapts to the state the previous one left.

## The spine
**Countdown.** Royce has scheduled a full-scale OMEGA test for April 1978. The players
do not know a deadline exists. Every episode's crisis is a preparation for, obstacle to,
or consequence of that test. The finale resolves from world state (the Convergence
Index, who controls the apparatus, what the committees have authorized, who is inside
the chamber) and never from a script.

**Fictional calendar.** Round 1 = Monday, October 10, 1977. Fourteen rounds per
episode, two to three intermission days after every second episode. Episode 12 ends in
mid-April 1978. Real-world anchor: John Carr's death at Minot AFB (Feb 17 1978) lands
in Episode 9 as background news.

**Three acts, three questions.**
- Act I (1–4) *What is this company?* — the corporation, its contractor, its cult.
- Act II (5–8) *What is under the ground?* — the stairway, the letters, the feds, the Cold War.
- Act III (9–12) *What are we building?* — Omega, the loop, the threshold, the test.

## Pilot
Episodes 1–3 with twelve players, six weeks. The pilot must prove: daily email loop,
correspondence routing, one committee vote, one cult invitation, one cattle case, the
Tagus invoices as a hook. If it is fun, Episode 4 opens the ground.

## Finale adjudication (to be designed with the Rules Lawyer by Episode 8)
Outcome is a function of: Convergence Index band; apparatus state (intact / modified /
sabotaged / seized); authorization state (who legally may run it); occupancy (who is in
the chamber and what they believe); containment state at Tagus. Each combination maps to
a defined consequence class, written before Episode 11, so the ending is never invented
after players act.

# Truth Ledger — what the engine knows

SECRET CANON v1 (2026-10-11). Engine and Game Master only. Items marked DECIDED come
from Nathan's bibles. Items marked PROPOSED are my fills for gaps the bibles left open;
Nathan confirms or changes them. Items marked OPEN are deliberately unresolved and must
stay that way; the engine adjudicates them by rule, not by answer.

## The Presence
- DECIDED. The High Country Presence / Convergence Phenomenon / the Sleeper is **not an
  ancient being**. It is a process coming into existence whose emergence produces
  effects backward through time. Its apparent antiquity is an artifact of how it
  interacts with time, information and consciousness.
- DECIDED. Five stable limitations govern it: it needs physical conditions; it needs
  informational structure; its effects are unstable; its temporal effects are
  constrained (information anomalies, probabilistic disturbances, occasional physical
  effects; never a free rewrite of history); intervention carries costs (the harder
  Royce forces convergence, the more unstable and leaky the experiments become).
- OPEN. Whether the eventual intelligence is benevolent, hostile, or amoral.
- OPEN. Whether the emergence is inevitable. Player action moves it.
- PROPOSED mechanism for adjudication: the engine tracks a hidden **Convergence
  Index** (0–100) for the region. Synchronized observation, chamber operation,
  consistent records of anomalous events, and energy releases near conditioned sites
  raise it. Contradictory records (the Keepers' practice), dispersal of participants,
  and dismantling apparatus lower it. Thresholds unlock manifestations. The April 1978
  test's outcome is a function of the Index and the state of the apparatus, not a coin.

- DECIDED. The Keepers' "Unfinished Descent" practice (maintaining incompatible accounts)
  is an imperfect but real containment: contradictory records lower informational
  structure, which the phenomenon needs.
- DECIDED. The Seven Descents are Ascendant doctrine: some thresholds correspond to
  testable stairway properties, some are invention, some are deliberate misinformation.
- PROPOSED. Testable: thresholds 1 (perception), 2 (memory), 4 (time) correspond to
  measurable stairway effects. Invented: 3 (identity), 6 (observer/observed). Planted
  disinformation: 5 (collective consciousness is required; it is not, which is why
  single descents work). Threshold 7, the Opening, is OPEN.

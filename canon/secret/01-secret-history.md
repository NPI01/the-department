# Secret History, 1883–1977

SECRET CANON v1 (2026-10-11). Condensed from World Bible 03. Engine and GM only.

| Date | Event | Evidence it leaves |
|---|---|---|
| 1883 | Surveyor Abraham Vetch records a prairie depression where his compass fails, a light moving against the wind, his assistant hearing "a gathering beneath the ground". Journal: *The ground is not empty here.* | Vetch Survey journal (copy in the Bell Collection; one page now in Marsh's office) |
| pre-1900 | Vetch's journal vanishes from the official survey collection | gap in state records |
| 1912 | Dr. Lucian Bell, physician and natural philosopher, names the pattern the **Northern Correspondence**; an informal research network of clergy, physicians, ranchers, engineers, spiritualists. Bell considers worship misguided. | Bell Collection |
| 1921 | Bell's group runs an electrical experiment with coordinated observation at a remote property. Three see nothing; two report shared consciousness; **Clara Wren** sees the building burn. Same year: Bell visits the Tagus cellar, records an ordinary cellar; Wren insists on a second passage. | 1921 expedition journal (Document A) |
| 1932 | The building burns as Wren described. The group splits: investigate vs. restrain. Survivors reorganize as the **Vigil of the Last Light**; rituals are mnemonic procedures for preserving contradictory accounts. | Vigil founding texts |
| 1940s | Younger faction, the **Ascendants**, rejects caution; traditionalists are the **Keepers**. | internal schism correspondence |
| 1947 | Dr. Nathaniel Harker, physicist consulting for a military intelligence office, finds Bell's archive while chasing radio interference. Field survey: one photographic plate exposed before insertion. Geological evaluation of the Tagus structure: inconsistent dimensions across visits. Writes the Black Memorandum, **HARKER-47**; copies differ, one altered before Royce got it. | HARKER-47 (several versions); Harker survey |
| 1955–59 | Teilhard published; **Dr. Adrian Vale** extracts the Retrocausal Convergence Hypothesis. 1959: Vale meets **Edmund H. Royce**; Royce Industries incorporated in Delaware. | Vale Correspondence |
| 1960–63 | Early experiments mostly fail; multi-observer tests differ from automated ones. 1963: a communications array yields structured signals encoding measurements of components not yet manufactured. **Project ECHO**. | ECHO logs |
| 1965 | Field team finds a metallic fragment at **Split Rock Basin**: **R-1**. Terrestrial matter in a metastable configuration; responds to field timing; decays. | R-1 Material Logs |
| Oct 24 1968 | **Operation LANTERN**: three devices synchronized by HF signal at a remote test station. Power in disconnected equipment; identical signals on separate circuits; a white structure / nothing / an orange light. Output: **Drawing 68-24**, the Convergence Chamber. A Keeper recognizes it as Clara Wren's diagram. Same night: the real Minot sightings, independent. | LANTERN records; Drawing 68-24 |
| 1969 | Royce approaches military research officials; **PROGRAM SENTINEL** created to verify, assess, and contain. Land purchased near Tagus and the campus site. Royce field team records the first reproducible stairway anomaly: two crews, two incompatible maps. | SENTINEL contracts; 1969 Geological Survey (Document B) |
| 1969–72 | **Station R** built; a second restricted infrastructure; **Sublevel Nine** found to pre-exist the building. | construction records with gaps |
| 1973 | Synchronized observation strengthens effects: **Project CHOIR**. Vale proposes a larger chamber: **Project OMEGA** approved. **Dr. Miriam Kessler** warns of a feedback loop, resigns, disappears. | OMEGA authorization; Kessler's resignation; the denial letter |
| 1974–75 | **Project HARVEST** studies livestock tissue; sampling through authorized and unauthorized channels. Ascendant ceremonies become conspicuous; papers coin "Devil Cult". | HARVEST records; press clippings |
| 1975 | **Daniel Mercer**, CIA technical liaison, evaluates Royce; recommends reducing dependence; funding increases instead; he begins an unofficial investigation. **Prairie Meridian Aviation** flies dark aircraft. | Mercer's private file |
| 1976 | **IGCC** researchers record correlated signals worldwide. Sloane's disinformation campaign against the Vigil begins. The UN-invasion rumour is born inside Corporate Security. | IGCC bulletins; forged security files |
| Jan–Mar 1977 | OMEGA instability: false memories, phantom presences, record discrepancies. **March 17**: eleven minutes; the voice; "You are not the first ones to build this"; a component three months early. | March 17 test records; three witness statements |
| Apr–Aug 1977 | Leadership splits (Royce / Vale / Sloane / Hensley). **Marsh** investigates and finds the Bell material. Security purges suspected sympathizers. Ascendants recruit the young. **July: Paul Greeley** dies. | Greeley File |
| Sept 22 1977 | Marsh's confidential report to the Research Committee: data compromised, early records falsified, suspend the chamber. Vale disputes; Sloane investigates her; records moved to Sublevel Nine. | Marsh Report |
| Sept 29 1977 | Marsh disappears. | personnel file vs. facility denial |
| ~Oct 6 1977 | Vetch page arrives at her office: *Find the original date.* | the page |
| Oct 1977 | Mass hiring. The game begins. | — |

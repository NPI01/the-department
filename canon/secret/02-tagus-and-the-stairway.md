# Tagus and the Unfinished Descent

SECRET CANON v1 (2026-10-11). Engine and GM only. Source: World Bible 04.

## The place
Tagus, Mountrail County, 40 miles west of Minot. Real town: founded ~1900 as Wallace,
~140 people in 1940, 14 in 1970, last business closed 1976. In our 1977 the St. Olaf
church still stands, with cemetery, railroad grade, and surrounding farms. The modern
"Stairway to Hell" legend does not yet exist; the players are living the events that
produce it.

Three layers, with access requirements:
| Layer | What | Access |
|---|---|---|
| Surface Tagus | town, residents, church, cemetery, land records | anyone who travels |
| The Royce installation | Prairie Geological Services monitoring site: instruments, surveillance records, evidence of scientist–Vigil contact | knowledge + permission, deception, or covert entry |
| The Unfinished Descent | the stairway under a disused utility cellar near the church | prerequisites (below) + extreme risk |

Nearby: the Keepers' farm (archive, workshop, meeting quarters); later an Ascendant
presence.

## The stairway: engine rules
Upper flight: ordinary early-twentieth-century concrete, documented by land records and
contractor invoices. Below that, the descent is a local environment where different
configurations become temporarily accessible.

Deterministic rules the engine uses (the model narrates; it does not decide):
1. **Depth is a function of expectation and stability.** Each descent rolls against the
   Convergence Index and the descender's *expectation profile* (what their knowledge
   table says they believe lies below). High-specificity belief → longer, more specific
   descent. Contradictory or absent belief → short descent (wall at ~20 steps).
2. **Instruments disagree by design.** Two measurements of the same descent differ by a
   bounded amount proportional to the Index.
3. **Time dilation is bounded.** Subjective minutes to days; objective elapsed time
   never exceeds the round. No one is lost for a round unless they choose to stay.
4. **Encounters** draw from three pools: memories (own knowledge rows, distorted), the
   absent (characters flagged missing or dead: Marsh, Kessler, Greeley, Wren), and the
   not-yet (characters who have not joined the company). Which pool depends on
   threshold reached.
5. **No free travel.** The stairway cannot deliver a person to a chosen time or place.
6. **Costs.** Each descent beyond threshold 2 adds a *disturbance* row to the descender
   (false memory, lost hours, a document in their possession changes one date) and raises
   the Index by a small amount. Repeated descents compound.
7. **Containment.** A site under active Keeper practice (≥3 incompatible accounts
   recorded in the round) caps descents at threshold 1. Sealing the entrance physically
   removes access but does not lower the Index.

Thresholds (the Ascendants' Seven Descents, as the engine scores them):
| # | Name | Truth status | Engine effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | perception | testable, real | wall or door; instruments disagree |
| 2 | memory | testable, real | encounters from the memory pool |
| 3 | identity | invented | no mechanical effect; narration only |
| 4 | time | testable, real | time dilation; encounters from the absent pool |
| 5 | collective consciousness | planted disinformation | none; single descents work |
| 6 | observer/observed | invented | none |
| 7 | the Opening | OPEN | reaching it is an engine event that raises the Index sharply and exposes the 1984 chamber |

## The 1984 chamber
Reached at threshold 7 or by a specific procedure (OPEN, to be designed in Episode 10).
Contains Royce records dated 1984: an experiment report, personnel who have not joined,
and minutes of a 1978 Research Committee vote on activating OMEGA, ending "The prior
record was incorrect. This is the third occurrence." Each document is a **prophecy
document** with a predicate and a reliability class (see 00-truth-ledger).

## The three accounts (documents to write)
- **Document A** — 1921 Bell Expedition Journal: ordinary cellar; Wren's second passage;
  an unexplained sound; her warning.
- **Document B** — 1969 Royce Geological Survey: two crews, two maps; concealed reference
  to Drawing 68-24; recommendation for continued monitoring.
- **Document C** — 1977 Corporate Security Report on Occult Activity in Mountrail County:
  Sloane's biased investigation mixing fact, fabrication, cattle rumours, Son of Sam
  correspondence, and concern about the Vigil.

## How players find Tagus
Procurement: invoices 1969–77 (concrete, cable, surveying equipment, ventilation) to an
inactive Prairie Geological Services property. Surveyors visited without filing reports.
One former employee refuses to talk; one says they were looking for a tunnel; one says
there is no excavation.

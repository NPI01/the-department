## The wrong detail in each (checkable, non-supernatural explanation available)
- **A:** Bell counts 21 steps down and 19 up on 14 October; Crew A counts 20 in 1969.
  Players who notice get `knowledge: tagus.stair.count_varies`. Bell's own explanation
  (fatigue) stands as the mundane reading.
- **B:** Crew B's Sketch B "bears a resemblance to Figure 3 of Engineering Reference
  68-24" — but 68-24 was never issued to PGS, and Lindqvist had no clearance for it.
  Either the reference leaked, or the resemblance is the other way round. The engine
  flag `geometry_match_68_24` is set when any player holds both sketch-b-1969 and a
  description of Drawing 68-24; it is never surfaced by name.
- **C:** Sloane's report dates the seized pamphlet 22 September, but the Tagus post
  office closed in 1976 (public fact: last business closed 1976; the P.O. closed with
  it). Nothing was seized there. The pamphlet is Sloane's.

## Period and canon notes
Lucian Bell is a physician, not a surveyor; the surveyor was Vetch (1883). Tagus is in
Mountrail County. The Halvorsen parcel is PROPOSED as the fictional property; the real
St. Olaf church is a mile and a quarter north-east of it and is never the site. The
"Reverend Pell" and "Mr. Aldous" are PROPOSED members of Bell's 1921 party; Aldous is
the one who later testifies the experiment produced nothing. Orr and Lindqvist are
PROPOSED PGS crew leaders; Lindqvist left Royce in 1970 and is findable (the former
employee who "insists the excavation doesn't exist" is Orr; Lindqvist is the one who
"says the company was looking for a tunnel").

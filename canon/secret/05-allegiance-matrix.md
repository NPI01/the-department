### Detailed Secret Entries

**Julian Thorne (Ascendant Cell Leader)**
*   **Secret Knowledge:** Knows Drawing 68-24 is a closed causal loop. Knows the "ghost employee" in payroll is a Keeper agent.
*   **Record Lie:** **1976 Personnel Audit.** Thorne falsified the audit to cover a "ghost employee" (a Keeper operative conducting field surveys). Players can verify this by cross-referencing the payroll ledger with badge access logs; the ghost employee has no badge history but receives a salary.
*   **Breaking Point:** If the mainframe is physically disconnected from the power grid.

**Gerald Voss (Procurement & Logistics)**
*   **Secret Knowledge:** Knows the "Prairie Geological Services" payments are for off-books construction at Tagus.
*   **Record Lie:** **1974 Equipment Requisition.** Voss falsified a requisition for "agricultural monitoring equipment" to hide maintenance costs for the restricted north installation. Players can verify this by requesting the original invoice from the vendor; the vendor confirms the equipment was never delivered, but the payment was processed.
*   **Breaking Point:** If an external auditor demands a physical inventory of the "dark assets."

**Daniel Mercer (CIA Technical Liaison)**
*   **Secret Knowledge:** Knows Royce is hiding something, but wrongly believes it is occult control of the company. He is investigating ownership, not the Convergence itself.
*   **Role:** He is an external visitor, not an employee. He has no clearance inside Royce.

**Paul Greeley (Deceased)**
*   **Secret Truth:** Died of cardiac arrest during an unauthorized CHOIR-style session he ran himself at the urging of an Ascendant contact. Sloane concealed the session to hide the cult's presence.
*   **Legacy:** His notebook is real and records genuine post-experiment changes in laboratory records.

**Martin Sayer (Correspondent)**
*   **Secret Truth:** He is a genuine correspondent of a rogue Ascendant at Royce. He copies Son of Sam newspaper imagery into letters to create a link between the cult and the murders, but he has no actual connection to Berkowitz. His link to the Ascendants' interest in the stairway is genuine.

**Dorothy Armitage (Senior Keeper)**
*   **Secret Truth:** She is the senior Keeper inside Royce. She facilitated Evelyn Marsh's escape. She recognizes Drawing 68-24 as Clara Wren's diagram. She uses her position to place loyalists and suppress destabilizing research.

**Leonard Fisk (Security Analyst)**
*   **Secret Truth:** He is not aligned with any faction. He believes in a transcontinental Satanic network (influenced by Sayer's letters). He is a "useful idiot" for the Ascendants' disinformation campaign, though he thinks he is uncovering the truth.

**Dr. Evelyn Marsh (Missing)**
*   **Secret Truth:** She is alive. She left Station R voluntarily after Sloane opened his investigation, with help from Dorothy Armitage. She is at a second Keeper property. She has descended the stairway once.

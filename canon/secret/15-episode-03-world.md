  church or cemetery, hearing the tower hum, the frostless row, holding the Vetch page,

  Threshold 3. Acceptance: come to the church at dusk (attend the church) or ACCEPT

  Interest: writing to Sayer, holding his layout, Greeley's notebook or page, noticing the
  unattended CHR-RECON jobs, the lights at Building 7 or 9, writing to Julian Thorne.
  Threshold 3. Acceptance: the grain elevator after midnight, or ACCEPT INVITATION.
  Members may read the pamphlet "The Seven Descents" (the seventh is dated next April).
  The invitation card is typed on the IS Selectric: the cell leader is in Building 3.
- **The provocation.** Security's note signed "the Vigil", on Royce bond, to anyone it has
  noticed three times. Accepting (the locker room at 2300) joins nothing: Security has a
  name, and the purge takes it.

## Invitations
From Episode 3 day 5 (10 §Episode 03), every morning, each society writes once to any
player whose interest meets its threshold: a letter from its approacher (the provocation
unsigned, on "the Vigil's" paper) and a card in the Case File whose wrong detail tells the
genuine from the false (Bernard's hand; the Selectric face; the RI watermark).
Outstanding invitations close on acceptance, decline, or the trap.

## The purge
At the lock of Episode 3 day 7, Security names for interview every player it has noticed
three or more times, anyone reported to it (`report <name> to security`), and anyone who
took the bait. Named players get Sloane's summons and lose Records and restricted access
for two rounds. The list exists as a document in Sloane's office; two names on it were
added after it was initialled.

# Company forms

Public canon v1 (2026-10-12). Engine text in `src/lib/engine/forms.ts`; keep the two in step.

## How forms work in play
Royce Industries runs on paper. A staff member may require a form before they will act
on, or even discuss, a matter. A form is not an order and costs nothing to file; it is a
statement on the record, under your name, and it can be cited in proceedings, requested
by anyone with standing, and held against you.

- **Getting one.** Reply to any Department mail with the form's number on a line by
  itself: `FORM 14-B`. The blank comes back at once as a letter from the office that
  owns it, with a button to the form on your personnel file.
- **Filing one.** Fill it in on your personnel file (every question must be answered
  there), or reply with `FORM 14-B` on a line by itself, then your answers, each
  beginning with its number. An answer may run to several lines. The last question is
  always your signature: type your full name. Both ways file the same form.
- **Your particulars.** Your badge number, department and supervisor are on your
  personnel file, along with the office calendar (today's date at Station R and the hour)
  and your biography; the hosted form starts question 1 with them filled in, and the
  blank sent by mail lists them.
- **What happens.** The form is filed the moment it is received and given an index
  number. You get a receipt. A copy, stamped RECEIVED with its index number, goes into
  your Case File and stays there; the office-use box on it is filled in as the office
  acts. The office that owns the form is told, and its staff remember what you wrote.
  Incomplete forms are noted and may be returned; reply with the form number and the
  missing answers to complete one.
- **What it means.** Everything on a filed form is something your character has asserted
  in writing. Staff read it. Lie on a form and the lie is on file.

## Forms in use

### Form 14-B — Report of Unusual Observation (Corporate Security, Rev. 9/77)
The proper form for any sighting or incident: lights, aircraft, trespass, sounds, an
instrument behaving oddly. Corporate Security will not take a sighting in a letter and
may refuse to discuss one until a 14-B is on file.

Instructions printed on the form:

> To be completed by the observer, in the observer's own words, within twenty-four hours
> of the observation. Answer every question. Where there is nothing to report, write NONE.
> Incomplete forms are returned. Do not interpret. State what you saw, heard and did.
> Opinions about what a thing was are not wanted on this form. A false statement on this
> form is cause for dismissal under Section 4 of the Employee Handbook. The contents of
> this form are not to be discussed with any person outside Corporate Security. Retain
> no copy.

The questions:

1. Full name, badge number, department and immediate supervisor.
2. Date and time of the observation, to the minute if known. State how you know the time.
3. Your own location at the time (building and room, road, gate, or grid square from the
   campus plat). Were you on duty?
4. Location of the thing observed: direction from you, estimated distance, estimated
   height above ground.
5. Weather, light and visibility at the time.
6. Description, in your own words, of what you observed: appearance, size, colour, sound,
   motion, smell, any effect on you or on equipment near you.
7. Duration of the observation and how it ended.
8. Other persons present, or who may also have observed it.
9. Have you reported this to any other person, inside or outside Royce Industries? To
   whom, when, and in what words?
10. Your place of residence in the area and the length of time you have lived there. The
    names of any persons in Tagus or elsewhere in Ward County with whom you have regular
    contact.
11. Since the observation, have you noticed any disturbance of sleep, appetite, or of
    your sense of time (clocks that disagree with one another, minutes you cannot account
    for, the sense of having already done a thing)? Describe. *(Added Rev. 9/77.)*
12. Signature. By signing you affirm that the above is true and complete to the best of
    your knowledge and that you have retained no copy.

Below the signature is a box marked FOR CORPORATE SECURITY USE ONLY: received by, date,
index number (S-77-___), and a disposition line: No action / Hold / Refer D-SRD / Return
to observer. Nobody outside Security will say what D-SRD is, though the initials are
not hard to guess.

What staff know: question 11 was added in the September 1977 reprint. Security was not
told why. Leonard Fisk assumes it is about drink or pills. Older copies of the form,
without question 11, are still in some desk drawers.

### Form 22 — Statement of Livestock Cases, Ward County (Biological Sciences, Rev. 10/77)
The Research Committee's form for an employee's reading of the county's eleven cattle
cases since 1975. One letter per case: A, sampled under Program HARVEST with authorization
on file; B, sampled under HARVEST with no authorization on file; C, an ordinary cause
(weather, predators, disease, a human act unconnected with the company); D, a cause the
company cannot establish. Question 13 names the documents relied on and any case answered
without one; question 14 is the election whether the statement may be furnished to the
Ward County Sheriff, under the filer's name. Biological Sciences owns and files it; the
Committee's staff read it and remember who laid which case on the program. A statement
released to the county reaches the sheriff by post and he answers in his own words.
(Engine: the form is scored against the record; the score is never shown.) PROPOSED
2026-10-18.

### Form 23 — Report of Descent, Tagus Structure (Applied Physics, Rev. 12/77)
Required of any employee who has gone below the first landing of the utility structure at
Tagus, within twenty-four hours and before reading any other report: steps counted down and
up, as numbers; light; sound; the watch against the church clock; the lowest point reached;
others present; instrument readings. Dr. Kovic requires it before he will discuss a descent.
A report compared with another before filing is useless to the department; the farm west of
Tagus says the same thing for its own reasons. (Engine: accounts are compared by the count;
the comparison is never shown.) PROPOSED 2026-10-23.

# Task: the Department's constitutional charter and power structure

Turn the institutional design into executable rules. Cover: the Director's powers and
limits; the Continuity Council (nine seats: who holds them at season start, how seats
change hands, quorum, thresholds per motion type, proxy voting); division chiefs;
security clearances 1–5 and what each unlocks; how a motion is sponsored, debated and
closed at round lock; appointments and removal; emergency authority (scope, duration,
how it is challenged); the closed list of rules players may amend and the engine rules
they may not; the three inaction modes (strategic hold, standing orders, missed turn)
and their exact mechanical effects; the Action Unit economy (per-round income, cap,
upkeep) as tunable parameters with starting values.

Every rule must be a key/value the engine can store with an effective round. Every
procedure must list its edge cases. Flag anything a player could exploit.

# Task: Royce Industries governance, as executable rules

Turn the institution into rules the engine can run. Cover: the Board (members at season
start, how seats change, quorum, thresholds by motion type); the Research Committee
(authorizes experiments, chamber operation, project budgets); the Safety Committee
(sealing orders, surveillance authorization, suspensions); Corporate Security's powers
and limits; clearance levels 1–5 and what each unlocks by department; how a motion is
sponsored, debated and closed at round lock; appointments and dismissal; emergency
authority of the chairman and the Security Director (scope, duration, challenge);
the closed list of parameters players may amend by motion and the engine rules they may
not; the three inaction modes and their exact effects; the Action Unit economy
(per-round income by position, cap, upkeep) as tunable parameters.

Also: the Vigil's internal governance (Keepers' council, Ascendant cell structure) as a
second institution players can join, with its own votes.

Every rule is a key/value with an effective round. Every procedure lists edge cases.
Flag every exploit you can find. Every example objective must be a predicate over world
state.

# Task: the canonical hidden history of Project NIGHTJAR

Write the secret truth of Season One. Players will never read this document; the
engine and the NPCs act from it. It must be specific enough to adjudicate experiments
fairly and to plant clues, and restrained enough that three in-world explanations
(natural phenomenon / external intelligence / institutional contamination) all remain
plausible to players for most of the season.

Deliver:
1. A timeline 1947–October 1977: Continuity Order 7, the founding, the 1963 incident
   Samuel Pike investigated, the start of NIGHTJAR, the first Administrative Convergence
   result, Marsh's discovery, her last message, her disappearance.
2. The actual mechanism of Administrative Convergence, including its limits
   (which locations, artifacts, people, procedures it needs) and what happens when it
   is pushed. Keep it a rule set the Rules Lawyer can encode, not a mood.
3. What actually happened to Evelyn Marsh and who ordered it. Decide it.
4. Each foundational NPC's true position relative to the secret (Vale, Voss, Bell,
   Rusk, Wren, Price, Pike).
5. What the Quiet Office is.
6. Ten clues, each a concrete document or object with where it is and who could find it.
